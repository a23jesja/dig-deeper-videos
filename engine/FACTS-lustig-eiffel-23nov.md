## Quotes by point

1. **1925, Paris**
   - MF: "In May 1925, an article appeared in a Paris newspaper about the decaying condition of the" [Eiffel Tower]
   - TIFO: "It was in May of 1925 when Victor Lustig first conceived the scheme"
   - F: Paris yes, the year not given.
2. **The tower's upkeep cost**
   - MF: "cheaper to tear down the Eiffel Tower than to fix it"; "the costs to maintain the Tower were simply too high"
   - TIFO: "The article remarked on the high cost of maintenance and repairs for the Tower, making mention that it was rusting."
   - F: "It was costing the city a fortune in maintenance and upkeep."
3. **False title**
   - MF: "the Deputy Director General of the Ministère de Postes et Télégraphes"
   - F: "Deputy Director General of the Ministry of Posts and Telegraphs."
   - TIFO: "proclaiming him the Deputy Director of the Ministere de Postes et Telegraphes"
4. **Fake stationery**
   - MF: "have a counterfeiter make him official government stationery"
   - F: "complete with his own stationery and business cards done up with an official French seal."
   - TIFO: "With documents and letterheads proclaiming him the Deputy Director"
5. **Dealers and hotel**
   - MF: "wrote letters to the five most prominent scrap iron dealers in the city."; "in the suite of a fancy hotel"
   - F: "The biggest scrap metal dealers in town were summoned to the luxurious hotel for a secret business proposal."; "the Hôtel de Crillon"
   - TIFO: "meet him at the Hotel de Crillon."; "Six dealers came"
6. **Tower coming down, metal for sale**
   - MF: "the government's decision to tear it down was controversial and so must remain hush-hush."; "the resulting 7000 tons of metal would be for sale to the highest bidder among them."
   - F: "winning the biggest scrap metal project in the country's history"
7. **Secrecy**
   - MF: "must remain hush-hush"
   - F: "the promise of complete secrecy from those involved."
   - TIFO: "This was a huge secret and the public could not know at this point"
8. **Poisson, new in town**
   - TIFO: "who was new in town and wanted to establish a reputation"
   - F: "Andre Poisson was relatively new to the area and trying to make a name for himself."
9. **Low salary**
   - MF: public servants "made a meager salary."
   - TIFO: "hardly making enough to pay his bills"
10. **Bribe**
    - MF: "Poisson understood that he was being asked for a bribe to secure the deal, and he obliged."
    - TIFO: "giving the Count fifty grand to make sure Poisson would win the bid"
    - F: "Lustig demanded a bribe for securing the transaction."
11. **Fled with the money**
    - MF: "Lustig, with Poisson's money in hand, fled for Austria."
    - TIFO: "Within an hour of receiving the money, the Count left Paris."
    - F: "By this point, Lustig had already fled the country."
12. **Too embarrassed to go to the police**
    - MF: "Poisson would be too embarrassed by how easily he fell for the ruse to go to the authorities"
    - F: "Poisson was so embarrassed about being taken advantage of" [that he never told the authorities]
    - TIFO: "decided to keep his mouth shut"
13. **Came back and tried again**
    - MF: "Six months later, Lustig returned to Paris and pulled the exact same stunt with five different scrap iron dealers."
    - F: "going back to Paris to try selling the Eiffel Tower a second time!"
    - TIFO: "he returned to Paris with the same letterheads and summoned five new scrap metal businesses."
14. **A dealer went to the police**
    - MF: "This time though, his mark went to the police, and the story hit the papers."
    - F: "The potential buyer went to the police before handing over the money."
    - TIFO: "He contacted the police."
15. **Fled to America**
    - MF: "Lustig soon fled Europe for the United States."
    - TIFO: "he abandoned the deal and fled in a hurry to the United States"
16. **Built for 1889, meant to be temporary (not used in the script)**
    - MF: "built as an entrance arch to the 1889 World's Fair and was never meant to be permanent."
    - F: "was only supposed to stand for 20 years before being disassembled."
    - TIFO: "it only had a permit to stay standing for twenty years, until 1909."

## Where the sources disagree (so these stay out of the voice)

- **The amount:**
  - W says "around 70,000 francs".
  - TIFO says "seventy thousand dollars" and "seventy grand", with "fifty grand" as the bribe.
  - F gives a winning bid of "250,000 francs".
- **How many dealers:** MF and TIFO's second round say five, TIFO's first round says six, and F gives no number.
- **Where he fled the first time:** Austria in MF (and W). TIFO says only "left Paris", and F says "fled the country".
- **7,000 tons of metal:** MF only.
- **"Sold twice":** the second attempt failed (MF, F, TIFO). The script says "tried again".
- **The hotel's name:** the Crillon appears in TIFO and F, but not in MF. The script says only "a grand hotel".
