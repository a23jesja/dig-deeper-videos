## Facts used in Part 1
- The collision was at about 11:40 p.m. ship's time on 14 April 1912. [S] [T] [NG]
- The ship turned but struck the iceberg along her side ("sideswiped"). [S] [NG]
- She had a reputation as "unsinkable". [T] [NG]  (The script says "they call her unsinkable", not that the builders claimed it.)
- She could stay afloat with four forward compartments flooded; the iceberg opened at least five. [S] [T] [A] [NG]
  (NG and S say six; A says five; the script says "at least five".)
- RMS Carpathia answered the distress call and arrived at about 4 a.m. [S] [C] [NG]
- The order was women and children first. [S] [L] [US]
- Twenty lifeboats with room for about 1,178 people; about 2,200 aboard: room for about half. [L] [S] [T] [US] [NA]
- The ship sank by the bow. [S] [NG]
