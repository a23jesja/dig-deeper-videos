About 140 words. If the voice track runs over 57 s, line 5 is cut first, then "Wrong man."

Left out on purpose because the sources disagree: how he got in (hid overnight or walked in on Monday), whether
the painting was under or wrapped in the smock while he walked (we show it wrapped, under his arm: WP and ME),
where the thumbprint was, and the exact arrest date (11 or 12 December 1913).

Real people (Peruggia, Picasso) appear only as our own stickmen. The Mona Lisa itself is a public-domain
painting from about 1503; the picture in the frame is our own painted version.
