# Alcatraz 1962: titel och beskrivning (utkast till STOPP 2)

**Titel:** Three Men Vanished From Alcatraz in 1962

**Beskrivning:**

On the night of June 11, 1962, three men dug through the air vents of their cells at Alcatraz, left fake heads in their beds and pushed off into the dark bay on a raft made of prison raincoats. They were never seen again.

The scenes in this video are dramatised illustrations, not footage. Facts from the Federal Bureau of Prisons ("Alcatraz", bop.gov) and PBS Secrets of the Dead ("Alcatraz Escape: June 1962").
