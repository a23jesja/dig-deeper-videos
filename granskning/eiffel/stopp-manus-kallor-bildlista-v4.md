## Beskrivning (utkast)

> Who Sold the Eiffel Tower in 1925
>
> In 1925 a con man named Victor Lustig pretended to be a French government official and sold the Eiffel Tower to a scrap dealer. Then he tried it again. This story largely rests on a single account: the 1961 book "The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower" by James F. Johnson, a former Secret Service agent. The scenes are dramatised.
>
> Sources: K. Edgington, "Victor Lustig", EBSCO Research Starters (2024), with its works cited (J. F. Johnson, The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower, 1961; A. Boese, Museum of Hoaxes, 2002; M. Farquhar, A Treasury of Deception, 2005; C. Sifakis, Hoaxes and Scams, 1994). B. DeMain, "Smooth Operator", Mental Floss (2020). M. Blitz, "The Man Who Sold the Eiffel Tower", Today I Found Out (2015). B. Carlson, "Lessons on modern financial scams from the man who tried to sell the Eiffel Tower twice", Fortune (2020).
