- **Note on line 10, "took days":** SCE says the ransom "took some days to be delivered". The Johnson passage on the delay
  (Marks and the boat held up by wind and current) I saw only in a search summary, not in the text. Treat line 10 as
  resting firmly on SCE, with Johnson to be checked.
- **Note on lines 1 and 15:** the ransom was medicine, but Johnson also says the pirates took gold and silver from the
  captured ships (about £1,500). The script never says he wanted nothing else; QAR calls the medicine "the pirates' main
  demand". If the reviewer finds "All that, for one chest of medicine" too strong, use "And the ransom? One chest of
  medicine."
- **Not said:** what the medicine was for (archaeologists found a syringe with mercury traces in his wreck, which is
  suggestive but not proof); "carousing" envoys (SCE only); the threat to send heads ashore; the number of ships; the
