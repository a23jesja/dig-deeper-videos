## Kapitel 4: Straffen (3:55-5:10)

**3:55** [SOURCE] First, forget the plank. [OWN: Sir Digsworth, med sågen] Allow me. [SOURCE] Pirates almost never made anyone walk it. The idea comes mostly from stories like Treasure Island and Peter Pan. If they wanted someone gone, they simply threw him overboard.

**4:10** [SOURCE] The real punishment was worse. Cheat the crew out of even a dollar, and you'd be marooned: left on a desert island with a gun, a few shots, a bottle of water and a bottle of powder. To survive... or starve. You watch one man rowed out to his island, with his bottle of water. [OWN] You decide you will never cheat.

**4:27** [SOURCE] Strike another man on board, and on one ship you'd get what they called Moses's Law: thirty-nine lashes on your bare back. The same ship banned smoking in the hold without a cap on your pipe. [OWN: kvartermästaren, am_fenrir, sätter locket på pipan] Cap. On.
