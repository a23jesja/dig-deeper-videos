## Källor (alla lästa i sidans råtext)

- **[R1]** Royal Museums Greenwich, "Pirates: fact or fiction?" (James Gill): https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/topics/what-do-pirates-do . "Pirates never made their victims walk the plank. They just threw them overboard or abandoned them on desert islands. 'Marooning' - deliberately leaving someone on an uninhabited island - was one of the punishments a pirate captain would have used to maintain discipline among their crew." Om flaggan: "The flags were designed to frighten victims into surrendering without a fight, allowing a pirate captain to gain treasure without risking crew in combat." Om ursprunget: "Walking the plank and its association with pirates seem to be due to its key role in J.M. Barrie's Peter Pan and its subsequent appearance in several pirate films." Om reglerna: "'If any man should lose a limb or become disabled,' the code supposedly stated, 'he is to have 800 pieces of eight, with smaller amounts for lesser injuries.'"
- **[R2]** Royal Museums Greenwich, "Why do pirates say 'arr'?": https://rmg.co.uk/stories/topics/why-do-pirates-say-arr . "Did pirates make people walk the plank? Rarely ... Whilst we know that walking the plank took place at least a few times, there aren't many documented instances. ... It would have been easier to simply push someone overboard if they needed getting rid of."
- **[N]** National Geographic History, Jamie L. H. Goodall, 3 maj 2022: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/history-magazine/article/pirate-portrayals-are-more-fantasy-than-fact . "If a pirate violated the code, it is unlikely they were made to "walk the plank." Little to no historical evidence exists to support that practice, which was largely pulled from fiction, including Treasure Island."
- **[J-R]** Charles Johnson, A General History of the Pyrates (1724), Project Gutenberg nr 40580, Bartholomew Roberts artiklar. II: "if they defrauded the Company to the Value of a Dollar, in Plate, Jewels, or Money, MAROONING was their Punishment. This was a Barbarous Custom of putting the Offender on Shore, on some desolate or uninhabited Cape or Island, with a Gun, a few Shot, a Bottle of Water, and a Bottle of Powder, to subsist with, or starve." IX: "If ... any Man should lose a Limb, or become a Cripple in their Service, he was to have 800 Dollars, out of the publick Stock, and for lesser Hurts, proportionably."
- **[J-P]** Samma bok, John Phillips artiklar (skeppet Revenge). 3: "If any Man shall steal any Thing in the Company, or game, to the Value of a Piece of Eight, he shall be marroon'd or shot." 8: "If any Man shall lose a Joint in time of an Engagement, shall have 400 Pieces of Eight; if a Limb, 800."
- **[J-flagga]** Samma bok: "upon seeing the black Flag hoisted, struck his Sail and came to, under the Stern of Teach"; "they hoisted the black Flag, and fired three Guns each, at the Pearl; she struck".
- **[T]** R. L. Stevenson, Treasure Island, Project Gutenberg nr 120, kapitel I: "Dreadful stories they were--about hanging, and walking the plank, and storms at sea"; kapitel XXXIII: "what brave men walking the plank blindfold".
- **[P]** J. M. Barrie, Peter and Wendy, Project Gutenberg nr 16, kapitel XIV: ""Now then, bullies," he said briskly, "six of you walk the plank to-night, but I have room for two cabin boys.""

## Vad manuset medvetet inte säger

- **Inte "aldrig".** Greenwich skriver "never" på en sida och "at least a few times" på en annan. Rösten säger "almost never recorded".
- **Inga namn på de få kända fallen** (1769, 1822, 1829): jag har inte kunnat öppna någon säker källa för dem.
- **Inte att pirater satte fångar i land.** Att lämnas på en öde ö var ett straff för besättningens egna, och i bild är det piraten som fuskat som blir kvar.
- **Inte att alla pirater hade samma regler eller samma belopp.** Rösten säger "they had rules" och det belopp som står hos Phillips (800 pieces of eight för en lem); Roberts artiklar har 800 dollar och Lowthers 150 pund. Skylten säger varifrån reglerna kommer.
- **Inte att böckerna hittade på plankan.** Rösten säger att plankan "comes from stories, like" de två; uttrycket finns belagt före dem, men inte som piraternas sed.
- **Hajen är sagans haj.** Ingen källa säger något om hajar; den finns bara i sagobilden i början, i vattnet när han faller i, och som en fena. Rösten nämner den bara i uppräkningen av vad berättelserna har.
- **Ingen kölhalning** (National Geographics nästa mening har jag inget stöd för).
