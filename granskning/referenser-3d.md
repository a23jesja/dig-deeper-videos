# The reference level for the 3D style (written 2026-11-23 from Jesper's four reference Shorts)

Described in our own words from screen recordings Jesper sent in chat. No stills, lines, designs or scenes from
them are copied or stored here. Use this as the measuring stick: for every shot of ours, ask whether it could sit
in one of these videos. Numbers are estimates from contact sheets at 2 frames per second.

## What all four share

- **A figure in focus about 90 % of the time.** Shots without a figure are short (under 2 s): a map, a sign, a
  vehicle in motion, an object close-up.
- **A new shot every 1.5-2.5 s** in the first 15 seconds, and something moving inside every shot: hammering,
  painting with a roller, a plane banking, a crowd running at the lens, a king tripping.
- **Rich 3D environments with real objects:** textured materials (stone, riveted steel, painted wood, planks),
  many small props that belong to the story, readable signs and objects that carry facts (a counter showing a
  number, a carved date stone, a map with model ships).
- **Flat cartoon characters in front,** sharp, thick outlines, big eyes, faces that act; they stand on the floor
  with a soft contact shadow.
- **Soft depth of field:** the background is clearly blurred in medium and close shots, sharp in wide shots.
- **One motivated light per scene** with a clear colour theme: warm sunlight on stone, bright daylight with blue
  sky, low sunrise backlight with glare, overcast grey-blue.
- **The camera always drifts** (slow push-in or slide, roughly 2-5 % of the frame per second) and cuts on action.

## 1. The builder on the castle stairs

| Shot | Figure height | Camera | Light and depth | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Builder at the steps | 60-80 % | slow push-in, slightly low | warm sun from the side, columns blurred | swings a hammer, then measures with a folding rule, smug grin |
| King climbs | 80-100 % (cut at chest or legs) | low angle, follows him | same warm light | steps up, trips on one step, falls forward |
| King reacts | 50-70 % | over the builder's shoulder (builder's back as a dark shape in the foreground) | sunlit arch behind | face goes red, then purple with small flames, shouts a line |
| Guards | 60 % | medium two-shot | darker corridor | one winks, the other stares |

Faces: very large expression changes (colour change, flames, sweat, grin). Props: stone steps with wear, columns,
banners, a hammer and a folding rule.

## How to use this in a STOPP

For each of our shots, write: figure height, camera move and speed, light and colour theme, depth of field, the
props in view, what the figure does and when something new happens, and which reference shot above it is closest
to. If no reference shot is close in level, the shot fails.
