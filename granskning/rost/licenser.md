README: "Licensed under MIT, Chatterbox has been benchmarked against leading closed-source systems like ElevenLabs". The five files used (ve.safetensors, t3_cfg.safetensors, s3gen.safetensors, tokenizer.json, conds.pt) were downloaded without any login.
- Watermark: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/resemble-ai/chatterbox/master/README.md: "Every audio file generated by Chatterbox includes Resemble AI's Perth (Perceptual Threshold) Watermarker - imperceptible neural watermarks that survive MP3 compression, audio editing, and common manipulations while maintaining nearly 100% detection accuracy." - Conditions: MIT attribution; watermark is always on (nothing in the licence forbids a monetised channel).
- Prompted variant: the only reference clip is our own synthetic Kokoro bm_george output (ref/kokoro-george-ref.wav in ~/voicetrial, 10.6 s). No real person is cloned. The default variant uses the built-in conds.pt voice that ships in the MIT repo; its origin is not documented in the README.
