# Why did Blackbeard put fuses in his beard?

## Description
Blackbeard's beard smoked in battle. Why would anyone do that? Watch him get ready for a fight. First he twisted his beard, which grew up to his eyes, into little tails with ribbons. Then he tucked slow-burning fuses under his hat, so they smoked beside his beard. Add six pistols across his chest. Now he looked like something out of hell. It was probably meant to make crews give up without a fight. Nearly all of this comes from one book, printed six years after he died. So the fire was a costume.

The scenes are dramatised, and the lines in the speech bubbles are our own, not quotes. The fuses, the ribbons and the six pistols are from Charles Johnson's "A General History of the Pyrates" (1724), the one book nearly all of it comes from.
