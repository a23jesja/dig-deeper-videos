Why did pirates wear eye patches? One popular idea: to keep one eye ready for the dark below deck. Our eyes need around twenty to thirty minutes to adjust to darkness, and MythBusters called the idea plausible in 2007. But nothing from the pirates' own time says they really did it.

The scenes in this video are dramatisations.
