## Description
Did gladiators really fight to the death? Not usually. About nine times in ten, a gladiator lived to fight another day. Each one was trained, fed and doctored, and that cost a great deal. The last thing the school's owner wanted was a dead one. There were rules, and a referee. A beaten man raised a finger to give up. And most of the time, he was spared. So no: most fights ended with both men walking out.

"About nine times in ten" is National Geographic's estimate; the share varied between times and places, and some fights did end in death. The arena doctor is Galen, who wrote that no gladiators died in the five years he cared for them (Archaeology Magazine).

The scenes are dramatised. "Do you know what you cost me?" and "Relax. They'll probably be fine." are our own lines, not quotes.
