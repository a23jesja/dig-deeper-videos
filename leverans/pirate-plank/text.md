## Description
Did pirates really make you walk the plank? Almost never, as far as the records show. Real pirate crews had written rules: pay for a lost limb, and an empty island for anyone who cheated the crew. Unwanted men simply went over the side. The plank comes from stories like Treasure Island and Peter Pan.

The scenes are dramatised. The rules shown are from the pirate articles printed in Charles Johnson's "A General History of the Pyrates" (1724); the amounts differed from crew to crew.
