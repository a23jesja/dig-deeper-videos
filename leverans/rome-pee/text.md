## Description
Why did Rome tax pee? The emperor Vespasian's treasury was empty. Laundries washed wool with urine, and he put a tax on it. When his son Titus complained, Vespasian held a coin under his nose and asked if it smelled.

The scene is dramatised. Neither ancient writer says where or when the conversation took place, or exactly who paid the tax.
